## 📘 Case Studies

This section documents end-to-end projects I’ve built, focusing not just on **what** was created, but **why**, **how**, and what I learned along the way.

Each case study covers:

- **The real problem being solved**: Identifying the pain point or inefficiency  
- **Design and architectural decisions**: Why specific technologies or structures were chosen  
- **Trade-offs and constraints**: Balancing performance, cost, and complexity  
- **Packaging, deployment, and distribution**: How the solution reaches the end user (e.g., Docker, CI/CD)  
- **Testing and validation approach**: Ensuring the system works as intended  
- **Key learnings and future improvements**: Honest reflections on the build
